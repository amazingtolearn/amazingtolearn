Our Story

Born from a Passion for Music in East Delhi

Amazing To Learn was founded in 2014 with a single mission: make quality guitar education accessible to every student in East Delhi. What began as small home batches in Laxmi Nagar has grown into one of the most sought-after music academies in the area — with students travelling from Nirman Vihar, Preet Vihar, Mayur Vihar, Patparganj, and beyond.

We believe music is not a talent reserved for the gifted — it is a skill anyone can learn with the right teacher, the right method, and consistent practice.

Teaching Philosophy

How We Teach

🎯

Goal-First Learning

Every student starts with a goal — a song they want to play, a style they want to master. We build the curriculum around that goal so practice stays motivated.

🔄

Repeat Until Right

We don't rush through syllabus. Chords, scales, and techniques are drilled until they become muscle memory — the foundation of real playing ability.

🎵

Songs from Day One

Students play actual songs from the first week — not just exercises. This keeps enthusiasm high and shows rapid progress to beginners.

📱

WhatsApp Support

Practice help is available between classes via WhatsApp. Students can send videos, ask questions, and get corrections without waiting for the next session.

👥

Small Batches

Maximum 5 students per batch ensures individual attention. Personal sessions also available for those who want completely customised learning.

📋

Progress Tracking

Monthly performance reviews and parent updates for younger students. Every student knows exactly where they stand and what to focus on next.
