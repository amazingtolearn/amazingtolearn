What You'll Learn

A Complete Acoustic Guitar Curriculum

Our acoustic guitar programme is designed for absolute beginners as well as players who already know basic chords and want to move into expressive acoustic vs electric guitar comparisons aside — the acoustic path builds the strongest fundamentals. Every lesson is paced to your progress.

  • Fingerpicking guitar technique — alternating bass, arpeggios and syncopated rhythms
  • Travis picking — thumb-independence exercises and full song arrangements
  • Capo techniques — transposing songs, capo-specific voicings and open-string tricks
  • Hindi acoustic guitar songs — popular Bollywood film songs adapted for acoustic solo performance
  • Chord melody playing — combining melody, bass and chord voices in a single guitar part
  • Acoustic blues and folk — shuffle rhythms, turnarounds and traditional song structures
  • Music theory for guitarists — intervals, the CAGED system, basic scale positions
  • Ear training — learning songs by ear and identifying chord qualities

Why Acoustic Guitar

The Most Rewarding Instrument to Start With

No amp, no pedals, no fuss — just you and the guitar. The acoustic guitar builds the strongest fundamentals and sounds beautiful right from the start.

🎸

Rich, Full Sound

Acoustic guitars produce a warm, resonant tone that is deeply satisfying to play and listen to. The body amplifies every nuance of your touch — making technique development feel immediately rewarding.

🎒

Portable & Versatile

Take it anywhere — practice at home, play at a family gathering, perform at college events. No electricity required. The acoustic guitar fits every setting, from bedroom practice to a campfire jam.

🎵

Perfect for Bollywood Songs

Most Bollywood chartbusters are composed on acoustic guitar. Learning on an acoustic means you can play your favourite Hindi songs authentically — fingerpicked intros, strumming verses and all.
