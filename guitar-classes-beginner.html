Course Details

What You'll Learn

Our beginner guitar course is designed specifically for those who have never played an instrument before. Not sure how to start learning guitar? We handle everything — how to hold the guitar, tune it, and place your fingers correctly — building up systematically over 3 months.

By the end, you'll be playing full Bollywood and easy Hindi songs for beginners with proper rhythm and confidence.

3 Months 3 Days / Week 45 Min / Class Age 7+ Online & Offline

📚 Course Modules

  • Guitar anatomy, parts & tuning
  • Correct posture & left/right hand position
  • Open chords: A, D, E, G, C, Am, Em, Dm — basic guitar chords guide →
  • Basic strumming patterns & rhythm fundamentals — guitar strumming patterns →
  • Smooth chord transitions & hand coordination
  • Your first Bollywood & English pop songs
  • Introduction to guitar tabs & notation
  • Simple fingerpicking patterns

FAQ

Beginner Questions

Do I need to buy a guitar before joining?

+

No. For the first few classes you can use our guitars. We'll guide you on which guitar to buy for beginners based on your budget and goals once you decide to continue.

How long before I can play a full song?

+

Most students can play 2–3 simple full songs within the first month. By the end of 3 months you'll comfortably play multiple Bollywood and English songs.

I have no musical background — is that okay?

+

Absolutely. This course is designed for people with zero musical background. We start from the very basics and move at a pace that suits you.

What are the class timings?

+

Classes run Monday–Saturday from 7 AM to 9 PM. We have morning, afternoon, and evening slots. Call or WhatsApp us to check current availability.

Can I attend online instead of coming to Laxmi Nagar?

+

Yes! All courses are available online via live Zoom sessions. The quality and curriculum are identical to in-person classes. Students from across India join online.

What is the fee for the beginner course?

+

Fees vary by batch type (individual/group) and mode (online/offline). Check our guitar class fees in Delhi guide for a full breakdown, or call/WhatsApp us for our current rates and student discounts.
