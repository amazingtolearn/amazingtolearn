A Note for Parents

The ideal age to start guitar is 6–8 years. Children who start early develop better musicality, finger coordination, and discipline. Most children who start at Amazing To Learn continue for 2+ years and develop a lifelong relationship with music.

Parents Questions

Kids Guitar Class FAQs

What is the minimum age to start guitar classes?+
We welcome children from age 6. Younger children (6–8) start on a smaller-sized guitar and focus on fun music games before formal technique.
What size guitar does my child need?+
Children under 8 typically need a 1/2 or 3/4 size guitar. We guide parents on the right size and budget-friendly options during the free demo class. You do not need to buy a guitar before the demo.
How do you keep kids engaged during guitar classes?+
We use songs kids love, music games, short exercises, and regular mini-recitals to keep classes fun and motivating. Progress is celebrated with sticker charts and milestone achievements to build confidence.
How often should my child practice at home?+
10–15 minutes daily is ideal for children aged 6–10. Older children benefit from 20–30 minutes. We give simple, fun practice assignments after every class so home practice does not feel like a burden.
Can my child take online guitar classes?+
Yes. Online classes work well for children aged 10 and above. Younger children tend to learn faster in the offline studio, but online is available for all ages.
