FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can complete beginners join the guitar classes?

+

Absolutely! Our beginner course is specifically designed for those with zero prior experience. We start from the very basics — how to hold the guitar, tune it, and play your first chord. No prior musical knowledge required.

What is the duration of the guitar course?

+

The beginner course runs for 3 months (12 weeks). Intermediate and Advanced courses are 4–6 months each. Classes are typically held 3 days per week, with each session lasting 45–60 minutes.

Do you provide online guitar classes?

+

Yes! We offer fully live, interactive online classes via Zoom. The curriculum, quality, and instructor are the same as our offline batches. Students from anywhere in India can join.

Do I need to own a guitar to join?

+

For offline classes, guitars are available at the academy during class time. However, we recommend getting your own guitar for practice at home. We can help you choose the right guitar within your budget.

What is the batch size for classes?

+

We maintain a maximum of 5 students per batch to ensure personalized attention and quality instruction. One-on-one private sessions are also available upon request.

Is there a free demo class available?

+

Yes! We offer a completely free demo class where you'll get to meet the instructor, experience our teaching style, learn some basics, and ask all your questions before deciding to enroll.

Teaching Method

Our Proven Teaching Approach

01

Foundation: Chords & Posture

Begin with proper hand position, guitar tuning, and fundamental open chords. Build muscle memory from day one.

02

Rhythm: Strumming Patterns

Master essential strumming patterns and develop a strong sense of rhythm through metronome training and song practice.

03

Song Practice: Real Music

Apply learned techniques to real songs — Bollywood, English pop, classics. Students play songs they love from the start.

04

Performance Training

Regular performance sessions to build confidence, stage presence, and the ability to play smoothly under pressure.

Learning Philosophy

Music is a Journey, Not a Destination

We believe every student has the potential to become a musician. Our philosophy centers around three pillars: Consistency, Creativity, and Confidence.

We teach not just the technical aspects of playing but also musical expression — how to feel the music and communicate emotions through your instrument.

🎵 Consistency over Intensity

15 minutes of daily practice beats 2 hours on weekends. We build habits, not just skills.

🎨 Theory Through Practice

Music theory is taught in context — you understand why something works by hearing and feeling it first.

🏆 Celebrate Progress

Every milestone matters. We celebrate small victories to keep motivation high throughout the journey.
